An entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261119**: one image that sums up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026.

The image is a newspaper-style front page, dated **Thursday 19 November 2026**, carrying 39 stories first reported that day. Three lead stories get the top of the page: the first-ever trilateral meeting of Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen at APEC; Anthropic's $1.4 trillion Nasdaq debut as ANT; and the worldwide launch of Grand Theft Auto VI. Below them, a two-column bulletin covers the day across regions and beats: the ICJ's Rohingya genocide verdict, the Kerch Bridge hit a third time, the widening Gulf war, Israel's ground push in Gaza, the Fed minutes pointing to more rate hikes, Nvidia halting chip shipments to China, India–Japan defence talks, Cyclone Akvan, Kenya's second Ebola case, and more — with a footer ticker for the Van Gogh auction record, Stacy Keach, PV Sindhu and the 76ers.

Every story was checked against a news report published on 19 November 2026 (Reuters, BBC, AP, AFP, NASA, the Federal Reserve, Sotheby's and others). The image is original artwork drawn in code by `render.py` — flat shapes, rules and type only, no photograph or copied artwork.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) validates `entry.png` — whole-PNG chunk CRCs, at most 1024×1024 pixels, at most 1 MiB — and copies it to `/output/entry.png`. The image itself is a 1024×1024 PNG under 1 MiB.
